For each sentence, enter 1 (yes) or 0 (no) in each column. The images are in images/.

- objective: Statement about something visible and verifiable in the image (Snyder: 'what you see is what you say').
- interpretive: Interpretation: emotion, state of mind, atmosphere, meaning, intention, or symbolism attributed to figures or the scene.
- evaluative: Value judgment or praise ('beautiful', 'masterful', 'powerful').
- spatial: Spatial orientation: position within the painting (left/right, foreground/background, top/bottom, clock positions).
- colour: Names a colour specifically.
- comparison: Comparison or simile involving something familiar.
- error: Detail that does NOT match the image (invented or incorrect). Describe the error in 'note'.
- selection: Information you consider essential for understanding the painting (Snyder: 'less is more'; what matters most).
